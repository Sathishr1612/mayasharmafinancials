Meet the Founder

Maya A Sharma

Financial Educator NISM Certified Research Analyst Chartered Wealth Manager Share Market Trainer TEDx Speaker Mentor

Maya A Sharma is a Financial Educator, NISM Certified Research Analyst, Chartered Wealth Manager, Share Market Trainer, Investor, TEDx Speaker, Finance Professional, and Mentor dedicated to making financial literacy, investing, and stock market education simple, practical, and accessible for everyone.

With extensive hands-on experience in the Indian stock market and financial sector, she combines practical market insights with structured learning to help individuals understand the fundamentals of investing, trading, wealth creation, and financial planning. Her practical teaching approach has empowered thousands of learners to develop confidence, financial discipline, and the skills needed to make informed investment decisions.

Maya specializes in stock market training, technical analysis, investment strategies, risk management, trading psychology, and personal finance, delivering real-world education that bridges the gap between theory and market application. Her programs focus on ethical investing, disciplined trading, long-term wealth creation, and independent financial decision-making.

Whether you are a beginner starting your investment journey or an experienced market participant looking to strengthen your knowledge, Maya's training provides practical strategies, actionable insights, and lifelong financial education designed to help you achieve sustainable financial growth with confidence.
